A short puzzle adventure played as the spirit of Nina, a dead singer, moving through three acts, one for each surviving member of her band, towards a last performance. Each act is that person's grief turned into a place. You sing to interact with things and become a swarm of butterflies to get around. It runs about an evening and it is about grief throughout.
