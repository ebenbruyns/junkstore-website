A pirate action game mixing metroidvania exploration with souls-like combat. You are Marcus "Rookie" Ramsey, shipwrecked on a cursed island and searching it for the rest of your crew, the Angry Mermaids. More than 80 enemy types and 16 bosses stand in the way, and Marks you unlock give new abilities for fighting and reaching new areas. The cast is fully voiced.
