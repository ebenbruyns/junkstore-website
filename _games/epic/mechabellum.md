A mech auto-battler where you place units and set your formation before each round, then watch the fight play out with no direct control. Between rounds you add units, pick upgrades from their tech trees and counter whatever the enemy brought last time. It is mainly played against other people in 1v1, 2v2 and free-for-all, with a solo or co-op survival mode too.
