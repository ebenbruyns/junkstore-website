A roguelite puzzle game built on falling-block stacking, where the pieces are forests, rivers, plains and buildings rather than plain shapes. Placing them well produces resources and troops, and your army then fights the enemy holding each region, with your castle taking the damage if it loses. Runs branch across a map, there is a deckbuilding side, and failed runs still unlock new content.
