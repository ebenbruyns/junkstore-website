An arcade shooter from Vlambeer where you fly a small fighter against waves of planes, battleships, submarines and enemy aces under a crimson and cream sky. Weapons, bodies and engines combine into more than 125 versions of your plane, and each mix also changes the soundtrack. Over 100 missions unlock the parts, and the whole thing is built around chasing high scores.
