An adventure that switches between a 3D world and 2D dungeons, played as Nova, a Nano Cleaner who shrinks into infected people to suck a substance called Nano Dust out of their bodies and minds. Outside you walk, drive and explore the cities, valleys and wastelands of an island called New Theland. Inside, the dungeons play as top-down 2D adventures with puzzles and combat tied to whatever that person is carrying around. It runs eight to ten hours or more. The first Anodyne is not required reading.
