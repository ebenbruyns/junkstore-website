A sci-fi adventure with a strong visual novel side, played as the last survivor of a colony on a planet about to collapse. By day you explore with the colony's troubled AI, solving puzzles and finding out what happened to more than two dozen colonists. By night you spend time with four holographic companions, and your choices lead to one of several endings. The art is in a 16-bit Sega CD style.
