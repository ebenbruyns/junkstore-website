A single-player card-battling role-playing game about being a ten-year-old in the 1990s. On her first day, Jess gets Mega Mutant Power Pets, the card game the whole school is obsessed with, banned outright. Then the mutants from it start turning up for real. You can rewrite the game's rules as you go, and there is a separate roguelike Gauntlet mode.
