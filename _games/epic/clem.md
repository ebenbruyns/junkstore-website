A dark puzzle adventure, which its makers call a Puzzlevania, set in a house that seems empty. You wake with only a voice for company and a notebook labelled CLeM, full of sketches of insects and notes on alchemy, and a request to bring it beauty. Crafting magical toys lets you reach new places and uncover secrets in rooms you have already passed through.
