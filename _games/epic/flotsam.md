A city-builder on a flooded world, where your town floats and is built from the rubbish drifting past. You gather plastic and driftwood, make fresh water, catch fish and keep your drifters from getting sick, then sail the town out to explore ruins, rescue survivors and recover old technology. Drifters have their own stories, and specialists unlock new buildings.
