A horse racing and riding game that starts with a rundown ranch you have inherited and are meant to bring back. You raise foals, manage a stable and race in story mode or online against other players, on courses modelled on real tracks. Cross country is a separate jumping discipline with its own global leaderboard, and you can bet on races you are not in. Breeding runs on a genetics system covering coats and lineage, and a Horse Creator lets you build one and share it with other players. Between races there are open areas to ride around, from the ranch to the Swiss Alps, plus a photo mode and a homestead to upgrade.
