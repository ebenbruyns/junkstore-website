An action role-playing game based on the tabletop game, played as Cahal, a werewolf who went into exile after losing control of his rage. He is pulled back into the fight against Endron, an oil company serving a force that is destroying the planet. You switch between human form for talking, wolf form for sneaking and a huge beast form for combat.
