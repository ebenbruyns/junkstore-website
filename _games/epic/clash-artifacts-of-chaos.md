A third-person action adventure played as Pseudo, a martial arts recluse in a place called Zenozoik, who takes a small creature known as the Boy under his protection and ends up hunted by the mercenaries of Gemini, the Mistress of the Artifacts. You fight with fists and speed, dodging, stunning, blocking and countering parries, with a switch to first person for certain techniques. Several martial arts styles are learned along the way and can be swapped between freely. Before a fight you can challenge an opponent to a game of dice called the Ritual, and whoever wins sets the rules of the encounter, from summoning a monster ally to filling the area with fog or shackling movement. It shares a universe with Zeno Clash and Zeno Clash II but is entirely independent of them.
