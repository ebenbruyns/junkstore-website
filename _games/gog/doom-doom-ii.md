The first two DOOM games in one package: the 1993 shooter that set the template for the genre and its 1994 sequel. Alongside both campaigns it includes the official add-ons, plus SIGIL and the new Legacy of Rust episode, for 187 mission maps in all. It adds online co-op and deathmatch for up to 16 players, an in-game mod browser and a choice of soundtracks.
