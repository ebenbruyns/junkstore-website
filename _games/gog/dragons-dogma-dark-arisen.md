An open-world action role-playing game from Capcom built around Pawns, AI companions who fight beside you and pick up habits from how you play. Pawns can be shared with other players online. Combat lets you climb onto big monsters like griffins and hydras to hit their weak points, there are nine vocations to pick from, and Dark Arisen adds a large underground area full of tougher enemies.
