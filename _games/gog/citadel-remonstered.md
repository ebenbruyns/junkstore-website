A remake of Citadel, a 1995 first-person shooter for the Amiga, rebuilt for modern PCs from the original maps and assets. You are the only survivor of a ship sent to a planet where an outpost, later a prison, became a lab for illegal experiments on people. Textures and enemies are updated, and setting graphics to low brings back the original look.
