A strategy and trading game set on the railways of 1800s America. You run a steam train, fitting out its carriages, hiring a crew and carrying goods and passengers between towns, with bandits attacking along the line. Problems can be settled by talking, paying or fighting, and the story runs through real events and inventions of the period.
