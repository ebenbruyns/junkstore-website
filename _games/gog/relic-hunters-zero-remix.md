A free top-down shooter in which a crew of Relic Hunters fight off an invasion of evil space ducks and turtles on an asteroid full of ancient relics. There are seven characters to play, unlockable weapons and relics, and dodging matters as much as shooting. It is a remaster of a popular free original, with local co-op.
