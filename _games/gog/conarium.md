A first-person Lovecraftian horror adventure that picks up after the events of At the Mountains of Madness. You are Frank Gilman, waking up in an Antarctic base with no memory of how you got there and nobody else around. Finding out what he and three other scientists were trying to do means exploring the base and beyond and solving puzzles as you go.
