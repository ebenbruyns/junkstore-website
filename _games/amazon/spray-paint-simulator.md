A first-person job simulator about running a spray-painting business in a town called Spatterville. Most of the work happens before any colour goes on: repairing the object, filling the damage, then masking off everything that should stay clean. Jobs run from kitchens to local landmarks to a giant robot, paint and tape run out mid-job, and finished work unlocks a Free Spray mode for repainting the town however you like.
