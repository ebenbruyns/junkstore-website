A mining roguelite set on an endless wall, where you pilot a giant robot spider and head into procedurally generated mines for resources and lost technology. Monsters attack both on the face of the wall and down in the mines. Between expeditions you upgrade the spider and your exosuit and meet new characters in the sphere city.
