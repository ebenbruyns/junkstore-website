Kia ora Everyone!

This week's been a bit of a slow slog. I've been updating tutorials and writing a couple of new ones, which takes a lot of time and isn't all that fun. The other job I've been dragging my feet on is a video tutorial to go with each one. I've got the footage sitting there, I just need to psych myself up to edit it, record a voiceover and get them published. Video isn't my forte and it takes me forever, but I will get it done. I've put it off long enough.

Other than that, not much to report. I got my car back and she's looking pretty good. The rear bumper and tailgate have been fully replaced, so it's all shiny and new. It's so nice having her back, not that we go anywhere with how busy work is, but it's nice to have the option.

On the gaming front, Eben is still making his way through [Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration](/games/epic/rise-of-the-tomb-raider-20-year-celebration/). I've been getting my nightly fill of [Spray Paint Simulator](/games/amazon/spray-paint-simulator/), thanks to last week's Amazon Luna (Prime Gaming) giveaway, and dipping in and out of [Caravan SandWitch](/games/epic/caravan-sandwitch/). [Clash: Artifacts of Chaos](/games/epic/clash-artifacts-of-chaos/) might join the rotation this week too. That one got me straight away and I'm looking forward to playing more of it.
