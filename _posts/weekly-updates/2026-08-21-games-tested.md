Kia ora Everyone!

Not much to report from this past week. If you saw my post a while back about my poor car getting damaged, it finally went in to the panel beaters on Thursday. I won't get it back until sometime Tuesday, but at least it'll be all fixed up when I do.

On the gaming front, Eben and I have finally gotten stuck into some games. Eben finished [Tomb Raider: Game of the Year Edition](/games/epic/tomb-raider-game-of-the-year-edition/) and has moved on to [Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration](/games/epic/rise-of-the-tomb-raider-20-year-celebration/), and he seems to be having a good time with it. Nice to see him getting some quality game time instead of working all hours.

As I mentioned last week, [Steelrising](/games/epic/steelrising/) gave me a jolt of energy for gaming again. Funny thing is I've barely touched it since. Instead I went back and finished [Echo Generation: Midnight Edition](/games/epic/echo-generation-midnight-edition/), and it was a lot of fun. I love the 90s feel and spotting things I remember from growing up. I kind of miss cassette tapes and video stores, although we still have the odd one in the smaller towns here in NZ. Seeing an old computer and floppy disks in there was cool too. Those were the days, and this was a nice way to relive those simpler times for a bit.

Not sure what I'll play next. Maybe [Caravan SandWitch](/games/epic/caravan-sandwitch/), or I might get back to Steelrising. Mostly I'm just happy to have the motivation to keep playing. If you've got a suggestion, send it my way. I'm open to pretty much any type of game, so let me know if there's something I should try.
