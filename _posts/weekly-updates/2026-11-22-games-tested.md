Kia ora Everyone!

Wow, it's been a minute since I did one of these posts, or any post for that matter. After our stint house and cat sitting for friends, we spent about a week with my parents, visiting and helping out with some jobs.

When we got back home, we had to adjust to living on the water and in a small space again. The weather wasn't all that great, so we took the chance to have a break from working on Junk Store for a bit. Eben and I caught up on some games we'd been wanting to play, plus a few movies and TV shows.

Then the weather turned it on and it felt like summer was upon us, so we jumped at the chance to get stuck into some boat jobs. We spent a few days 'playing' in the mud on the tidal grid, cleaning and painting antifoul (3 coats), then moved on to 3 coats of topside paint. We knocked off a few other jobs too. I antifouled and painted the inside of the dinghy, cleaned up and varnished our rowing oars, our massive sculling oar and our tiller, and scrubbed our teak decks to get rid of a lot of built-up dirt. Plus a bunch of other jobs thrown in for good measure.

We're now ahead of schedule on the boat jobs we're aiming to get done this summer, but there are still plenty we want and need to do before winter. Fingers crossed we can tick everything off the list this time around!

Anyway, I hope you've all been well and enjoying the awesome games that have been released and given away since my last post.
