Kia ora Everyone!

If you missed the announcement, the [Junk Store Pro user manual](/docs/user/) is now up on the website. So if there was something you were looking for, or you wanted to know whether Junk Store Pro could do a particular thing, it's all documented now. The downside is that it's already out of date with what Eben has been working on while I was reading through it and getting screenshots. Ah well, now it's just a matter of keeping it current and using it to keep the tutorials up to date.

That leads into my next job: more documentation of a kind, updating the tutorials. I made a start on that yesterday, so I'm hoping to get the first of them up in the next day or so. I also recorded some videos that I need to edit and finish off so I can get those up too. The aim is to have a written guide and a matching video for everything, so you can use whichever suits you. It's a big undertaking, so I'll work my way through what I've already got written first, then move on to new guides.

Besides all that, we had more rain come in over the weekend, so it was an inside one here in NZ. It did bring a surprise though, we spotted a double rainbow and had to go out and admire it and take photos. Today's the opposite, clear skies and sunny, so it's looking like a good one. Mimi, our friends' cat we're looking after, has been keeping me company while I work, like she does most days.
